About

I studied Graphic Design and Photography at Central St Martins, but later found my voice through writing. I now have over 16 years experience in the Creative advertising industry and have worked on everything from big, global brand platforms, to pioneering social-first campaigns.

I really love what I do and have been fortunate enough to enjoy a career filled with brilliant partners, brave clients, ambitious agencies and shiny awards.

I've spent the last chunk of my life as a CD/ECD, but I still like to get heavily involved in the work and have a passion for mentoring and educating young creative people too.
